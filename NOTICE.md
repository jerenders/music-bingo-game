# Notice and acceptable use

**Music Bingo is for personal, non-commercial use only.**

Use it for family, friends and other free, private gatherings. Do not use it:

- at paid or ticketed events, or any event run for profit;
- at bars, restaurants, venues or businesses, even if the game itself is free;
- as part of a product, service or paid offering.

## Why

- **YouTube.** The game plays songs through YouTube's embedded player. YouTube's Terms of Service do not allow using embedded videos this way in commercial settings. You are responsible for following them.
- **Music rights.** The recordings and compositions belong to their artists, labels and publishers. This project does not host, copy or distribute any music or video. It only stores song titles, artists, release years and links (YouTube video IDs) to videos that other people uploaded. Public performance of music at a business generally needs licenses from the rights holders.
- **A business version** would need a properly licensed music source instead of YouTube. This project doesn't include one.

## Ads

Videos play in YouTube's own player. If you host from a browser signed in to YouTube Premium, clips play without ads. Without Premium, ads can appear and delay a clip.

## Accuracy

Song lists were assembled by hand, from memory and from public bingo cards. Artists, years and movie names may be wrong. Videos are third-party uploads, some are alternate or edited versions, and they can be removed at any time. Clips were not listened to in full, so check them yourself, especially for events with children.
